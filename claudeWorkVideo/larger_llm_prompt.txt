Create a structured video narration plan from the following input.

Video brief: [Describe the video.]
Audience: [Who will watch it?]
Total duration in seconds: [Enter 15–600.]
Closing duration in seconds: [Enter 0 to disable, or 1–30.]
Closing narration: [Enter the message, or leave empty for silence.]
Required facts and wording: [Provide facts, names, dates, figures and exact phrases.]

Use strictly professional English. Preserve the supplied facts. Avoid filler,
invented specifics and unresolved placeholders.

Choose a dynamic number of main scenes based on the duration and content, from
1 to 40. Every scene must explain a coherent point. Allow enough time for
comfortable speech and reading. Use at least 1.6 seconds per scene and at least
9 seconds of main content.

Subtract closing duration from total duration to obtain main_content_seconds.
Assign target_seconds to each scene so their sum equals main_content_seconds.
Start scene numbers at 1. Set scene_count to the actual number of scenes.

Write natural, ready-to-speak narration, initially aiming for about 120–140
spoken words per minute of main content. Treat this as an estimate. The video
generator will measure actual audio and can shorten wording or make a small
speed adjustment to keep the final runtime within ±3 seconds.

For each scene provide a purpose, key_points, exact narration, slide_text_guidance
and top_right_header_guidance. Gemma will write the final slide heading,
description, supporting text and top-right header from these inputs.
The header can be a phrase or short sentence. It may repeat across related
slides or vary as the subject changes. The renderer reserves a separate header
area with clearance from the Savvy Asia logo.

Return valid JSON only, without Markdown. Use exactly the structure below.
Replace all sample values, including zeros, using the actual input. Include
one scene object for every scene. Use an empty closing narration when its
duration is zero.
